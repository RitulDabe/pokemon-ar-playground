⚡ Pokémon AR Playground
WEB AR DEMO

Bring your
Pokémon to life.

Place a playful 3D Pikachu-inspired buddy and a Charmander-inspired buddy on your real floor using your phone camera.

For best results, use a compatible Android phone in Chrome over HTTPS.
Camera permission is requested only when you start. This demo uses simple, original primitive-based fan models rather than official Pokémon game assets.
Move your phone slowly to help the device detect the floor.
Move your phone to find a surface, then tap “Place Pokémon”.
